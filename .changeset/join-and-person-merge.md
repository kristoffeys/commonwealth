`commonwealth join <git-remote> [--brain <dir>]` clones a team brain and routes every git org its project registry lists to it (an org with an existing rule keeps it), then imports the brain's shared rules. `commonwealth person merge <keep-id> <drop-id>` folds a second contributor identity (another email) into the first: notes are re-attributed and both emails resolve to one person from then on (`attribution_aliases`).
